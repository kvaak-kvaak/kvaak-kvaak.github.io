Browser Scroll Lab

Small, reproducible test pages for investigating visually jerky scrolling on Sailfish OS and comparing it with Android AppSupport. No external scripts, fonts or trackers.

Start with the “Absolute minimum” Test 01 — Clean in all four browsers. Film it at the same zoom and orientation. Then repeat with other tests. Clean means no diagnostics script; Measured adds a recorder HUD that itself can affect performance.

For the recordings, compare Sailfish Browser (Gecko), Atlantic (WPE WebKit), RooTitanium (QtWebEngine) and Android Brave. Use a second camera to capture visible motion and on-device FPS overlays. Do not interpret rAF callbacks as presented display frames.

Recording protocol

One camera, same physical phone, fixed screen brightness and browser zoom. Record a 3-second still view, a slow sustained drag, three flicks and deceleration, then another still view. Repeat twice per browser, reversing order. Compare clean runs first.

Measured mode: tap Start, perform the gestures for 15–25 seconds, then Stop and export JSON. An Android file-download restriction may prevent saving; the report can also be viewed in the expandable text field.

v1.0 · This is a browser-level diagnostic, not a GPU or Wayland frame-presentation tracer. All content is bundled and works without an internet connection once hosted or copied locally.
